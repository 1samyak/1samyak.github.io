Samyak Jain_

B.Tech Data Science · Quantum University · Roorkee

I build research-driven software at the intersection of AI, finance, and data. My work moves between product engineering, machine learning, and the details of how information is extracted, tested, and turned into decisions.

I'm currently building EQ-T, an AI-native equity intelligence platform under SARETY. The focus is on making company research more structured and evidence-led: connecting filings and financial data, testing accounting quality, and turning the findings into a clear investment thesis.

I enjoy taking a problem from an initial research question through data, system design, and a working interface. I am especially interested in financial intelligence, applied ML, retrieval systems, and tools that make complex information easier to inspect.
